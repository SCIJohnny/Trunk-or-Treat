2026 Texas Roadhouse x SCI Roofing & Remodeling Trunk or Treat — Official Rules

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A purchase will not improve your chances of winning.

1. Sponsor

SCI Roofing & Remodeling ("Sponsor"), Anna, Texas. (214) 578-0850.

2. Eligibility

Open to legal residents of Texas who are 18 or older at the time of entry. Employees of Sponsor and their immediate household members are not eligible.

3. Entry period

Entries are accepted from 5:30 PM until 8:00 PM Central Time on Monday, October 26, 2026, at the Trunk or Treat event at Texas Roadhouse in Anna, Texas. Entries received after 8:00 PM will not be accepted.

4. How to enter

  • 1 entry: complete the entry form with your first and last name, email, and phone number.
  • +1 entry each (up to 3): follow Sponsor on TikTok, Instagram, and/or Facebook and provide your username. Follows are verified before winners are confirmed; unverified follows do not count.
  • +4 entries: refer a friend or family member for a free roof inspection by providing their name, phone, and address. One referral per entrant.
  • +7 entries: request a free roof inspection of your own home. Free, with no obligation to buy anything.
  • +4 entries: request a free Christmas lights installation quote for your home. Free, with no obligation to buy anything.

Maximum 19 entries per person. One form submission per person; duplicate submissions using the same email or phone will be removed.

5. Prizes

Two (2) winners will each receive one (1) $100 gift card. Approximate retail value: $100 each. No cash alternative. Winners are responsible for any taxes.

6. Drawing and announcement

Two winners will be selected in a random drawing from all eligible entries; one person can win only once. Winners will be announced Tuesday, October 27, 2026 at 2:00 PM Central on Sponsor's Facebook and Instagram pages and contacted by phone and email. If a winner cannot be reached or does not respond within 7 days, an alternate winner may be drawn. Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received.

7. Privacy

Information you submit is used to administer this giveaway and, as you agreed on the form, to contact you about the giveaway, Christmas lights, and roofing services. You can opt out of texts by replying STOP and out of email using the unsubscribe link. Sponsor does not sell your information.

8. General

This giveaway is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by, or associated with, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, or Meta. Event hosted in partnership with Texas Roadhouse. Sponsor may disqualify any entry it finds fraudulent or in violation of these rules. Void where prohibited.
